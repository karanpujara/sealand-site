Key Services

SEALAND SHIPPING offers services like:

Ocean Freight Forwarding

FCL

FCL Import & FCL Exports, both products offer feathers like schedule flexibility, frequency of sailing per port-pair, reach to remote inland destinations and availability of equipments. From stuffing of containers to de-stuffing it at final desired destination of consignee, we can handle efficiently entire chain of activities. We have core competency into export from India to USA

LCL

A competitively priced consolidation product, LCL offers the highest levels of schedule integrity. We can offer LCL/FCL consolidation, LCL/LCL consolidation or Buyer consolidation whichever fits your requirements. This product provides you with reliable performance and a lower supply chain cost.

Custom Clearing & Value Added Services

SEALAND SHIPPING offers you error free handling of your export shipment such like Custom Clearance, Fumigation, Phytosanitary Certificates, Warehousing, Stuffing, and Assistance in Drawback claims, DGFT Related work, ISF Filling / AMS Filling for export to USA. We can offer such services through our dedicated service partners.

Supply Chain Solutions

Demand for Supply Chain Solutions (SCS) is on the rise; companies know the cost and time savings available through closer analysis of their processes for moving products.

Our Supply Chain Solutions team has one aim: to build better supply chains. It deploys tailored solutions that are easily implemented and set new standards for supply chain efficiency.

Air Freight Forwarding

Our global air freight services with network of partners, combined with road feeder service or ocean freight connections, offer you door-to-door delivery anywhere in the world. This comprehensive service is backed by the expertise of our local teams, who apply their up-to-the-minute insights on local conditions to reduce any risk of delays.

Industrial Export Packing

SEALAND SHIPPING offers you best solution for your export packing requirements i.e. palletisation, Wooden Crate, Plastic Bags, Wooden Box, Corrugated Cartoons, Lashing etc

Road Services

SEALAND SHIPPING offers you reliable and time-definite door-to-door transport. We are specialized in developing and operating customer-specific trucking services for distribution or inbound logistics. Performance control through control tower structures and defined key performance indicators, supported by our IT solutions, ensure an optimal material flow within your supply chain. By connecting our locations, we are able to offer you tailor-made solutions for full truck or less than full truck cargo loads.
